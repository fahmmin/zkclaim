# The Claim: zkClaim film, final shooting script

Running time 1:55, or 2:03 with the optional beat. The dialogue is in Hindi with English subtitles, and it stays free of tech talk: only the end card names the technology. All people and companies are fictional.

## Script

### Scene 1: The rejection (0:00–0:25), Room 2

**Shot 1, wide.** Verma behind a desk with stacked files and a rubber stamp. Rohan enters, a file in his hand.

> **ROHAN:** Sir, angioplasty ke baad hospital ne saare documents bhej diye the. Claim abhi tak pending hai. Jab tak payment clear nahi hota, hospital discharge nahi kar raha.\
> *Sir, the hospital sent all the documents after my angioplasty. The claim is still pending. The hospital won't discharge me until the payment clears.*
>
> **VERMA** *(not looking up)*: Complete medical file chahiye. Saari reports, diagnosis, prescriptions. Uske bina verify nahi hoga.\
> *I need the complete medical file. All the reports, the diagnosis, the prescriptions. It can't be verified without them.*
>
> **ROHAN:** Sir, sab bhej diya hai. Ab aur kya chahiye?\
> *Sir, everything has been sent. What more do you need?*
>
> **VERMA:** Verification ke bina payment release nahi hogi. Next.\
> *No payment is released without verification. Next.*

**Shot 2, close-up.** Rohan's hands tighten on the file.

> **ROHAN** *(quiet, voice breaking)*: Main apni poori medical history har table pe kyun rakhun? Mera claim hai... meri life story nahi.\
> *Why should I put my whole medical history on every desk? It's my claim... not my life story.*

**Shot 3.**

> **SECURITY:** Sir, please. Bahar chaliye.\
> *Sir, please. Step outside.*

The door closes. The music drops to silence.

### Scene 2: A different way (0:25–0:50), Room 1

**Shot 4.** Rohan sits near the desk. Anita notices him.

> **ANITA:** Rohan ji? Kya hua?\
> *Rohan ji? What happened?*
>
> **ROHAN:** Insurance wale meri saari medical details maang rahe hain. Uske bina claim verify nahi karenge.\
> *The insurance people want all my medical details. They won't verify the claim without them.*
>
> **ANITA:** Unhe aapki medical details ki zaroorat hi nahi hai. Unhe sirf ye jaanna hai ki claim valid hai.\
> *They don't need your medical details at all. They only need to know the claim is valid.*
>
> **ROHAN:** Toh woh prove kaise hoga?\
> *Then how do you prove that?*
>
> **ANITA** *(turns her laptop toward him)*: ZKClaim se. Hospital prove karega ki aapka claim policy ki har shart poori karta hai, bina aapki report dikhaye. Bas aapki permission chahiye.\
> *With zkClaim. The hospital proves your claim meets every condition of your policy, without showing your reports. We just need your permission.*
>
> **ROHAN:** Matlab meri diagnosis unko nahi dikhegi?\
> *So they won't see my diagnosis?*
>
> **ANITA:** Nahi. Na diagnosis, na reports, na dawaiyan.\
> *No. Not your diagnosis, not your reports, not your medicines.*

### Scene 3: The proof (0:50–1:15), Room 1

The inserts in this scene are Demo mode screen recordings.

**Shot 5, phone insert (Patient app).** Rohan opens his Kestrel Mutual policy card (Rohan Iyer) and taps **Show to hospital**; the QR code fills the screen. Then a laptop insert: Anita admits him on the Hospital desk with his card code.

**Shot 6, phone insert.** The screen reads **Consent to this claim?** and "**Your diagnosis never leaves this phone.** Kestrel Mutual gets a proof that the claim is valid. Not your records." His thumb hovers for two seconds, then taps **Consent to this claim**.

**Shot 7, laptop insert (Hospital desk).** Anita clicks **Prove & submit claim**. The checklist ticks one by one: Approved hospital, Policy issued, Policy active, Record anchored, Procedure covered, Within limit, Patient consent, Never claimed. Then the **PAID** stamp.

> **ROHAN:** Bas? Itna hi?\
> *That's it? That's all?*
>
> **ANITA** *(smiles)*: Itna hi. Claim settle ho gaya. Payment seedha hospital ko aa gaya. Aapko kuch nahi dena.\
> *That's all. The claim is settled. The payment went straight to the hospital. You don't have to pay anything.*

Rohan breathes out.

### Scene 4: The reversal (1:15–1:45), Room 2

**Shot 8.** Verma's laptop pings. He frowns and leans in. Insert (Insurer desk): the new settled claim. **Insurer sees**: the amount, the policy fingerprint, the nullifier, the hospital-list root, who it was paid to, and the time. **Insurer never sees**: diagnosis, procedure, prescriptions, lab results, medical record. He scrolls, looking for a reject button. Insert: "There is no reject button. Settled by the contract. A valid proof can't be denied."

**Shot 9.** Rohan walks in, calm now.

> **VERMA** *(slower, re-reading)*: Aapka claim... settle ho chuka hai. Hospital ko payment chala gaya.\
> *Your claim... has already been settled. The payment has gone to the hospital.*
>
> **ROHAN:** Aur meri reports?\
> *And my reports?*
>
> **VERMA** *(a pause; his eyes go to the "never sees" column)*: Maine dekhi hi nahi. Zaroorat hi nahi padi.\
> *I never even saw them. I didn't need to.*

**Optional beat, 8 seconds (cut it if the film runs long).**

> **VERMA** *(on the phone, to the hospital)*: Ek baar phir submit karo. Cross-check ke liye.\
> *Submit it once more. To cross-check.*

Cut to Anita submitting it again: the Tamper Lab's **Replay a paid claim**, which stamps **Rejected by math** with "Claim already settled".

> **VERMA** *(quietly)*: Duplicate bhi nahi chalega... theek hai.\
> *Even a duplicate won't go through... fine.*

**Shot 10, phone insert (Patient app, audit trail).** No one has opened his records; the app says "Nobody has opened your records." Edit caption: **Records viewed by the insurer: 0**.

**Shot 11.** Rohan nods to Verma and walks out into daylight. Hold two seconds on his face so the relief lands.

### Scene 5: End card (1:45–1:55)

Black screen, three lines with a beat between them:

**Your claim is not your medical history.**

**Why should proving one mean revealing the other?**

**ZKCLAIM: Prove the claim. Protect the patient.**

Small text: Zero-knowledge proofs on Midnight · Hackasol 5.0

## App actions, in recording order

Use the demo controls (the round flower button at the bottom right): **Reset everything**, then **Seed the story**. The seed issues Rohan's Kestrel Mutual card, anchors his record and leaves his consent waiting. Keys 1 to 6 switch roles. Demo state lives in the page, so do not reload during a take.

**Phone, 390×844:**

1. **Reset everything** → **Seed the story**. It opens the Patient app.
2. Open the card → **Show to hospital** → shot 5.
3. **Consent to this claim**, with the two-second hover → shot 6.
4. Off camera: Hospital desk (3) → **Prove & submit claim**. Then Patient app (2) → the audit trail, still empty → shot 10.

**Laptop, 1440×900:**

1. **Reset everything** → **Seed the story** → Patient app: **Consent to this claim** (off camera).
2. Hospital desk (3): admit Rohan with his card code (**Scan Rohan's phone**, or paste the code and **Admit**) → shot 5.
3. **Prove & submit claim** → the checklist → **PAID** → shot 7.
4. Insurer desk (1): the settled claim → shot 8.
5. Optional beat: Tamper Lab (6) → **Replay a paid claim** → **Rejected by math**, "Claim already settled".

## Recording checklist

**Screens**

- [ ] Phone inserts at a 390×844 browser viewport, laptop inserts at 1440×900 (DevTools device toolbar, 100% zoom). If the recorder allows, capture at 2× for sharp text.
- [ ] A clean browser profile: bookmarks bar hidden (Ctrl+Shift+B), one tab, no other extensions visible, zoom 100%.
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on. Desktop icons and taskbar hidden, or record the window only.
- [ ] Cursor highlight on. Move slowly and pause about a second before each click.

**Data**

- [ ] Demo mode, **Reset everything**, **Seed the story**, then follow "App actions, in recording order". Names on screen must match the cast table.
- [ ] Only fictional data on screen. No recovery phrases, passwords or real addresses.
- [ ] No money amounts in the dialogue; the app shows amounts in NIGHT.

**Camera, sound and edit**

- [ ] Shoot 16:9 landscape.
- [ ] Record dialogue clean (a lavalier or boom), plus 30 seconds of room tone in each room.
- [ ] Burn in English subtitles from the italic lines above, and export them as an `.srt` too.
- [ ] Keep each insert as its own clip with two seconds of handle at both ends.
- [ ] Before export, check every on-screen claim against [architecture.md](architecture.md). The film must not claim more than the code does.

## Where the screens differ from the approved draft

The draft script described two inserts differently from the app. The shots above follow the app, because the inserts are recordings of it:

- **Shot 7:** the app's checklist has eight ticks, including "Record anchored", and says "Procedure covered" rather than "Covered".
- **Shot 8:** the never-sees column no longer lists "which hospital" or "patient identity". The payout address is public, and the insurer knows whose policy each commitment is ([threat model](threat-model.md) L6 and L8), so the app dropped both and added "Lab results". The sees column also shows "Paid to".
